# NCC Tiles - Official Storefront User Manual

Welcome to the **NCC Tiles** digital storefront operations manual. This document is written specifically for business owners, sales administrators, and staff. It explains exactly how your customers will experience buying tiles on your website, and how you will manage those sales behind the scenes. 

There is no coding or technical jargon required to operate this system—it is built to be as simple and intuitive as managing a physical storefront.

---

## Part 1: The Customer Experience (How shoppers use your site)

When a customer arrives at your website, they are greeted by a professional, beautifully designed storefront showcasing your high-quality products. Here is the exact journey they take from browsing to paying:

### 1. Exploring the Catalog
* **Product Discovery:** Customers can browse through an entire digital warehouse of products, categorized neatly into **Roof Tiles, Floor Tiles, Wall Tiles, and Accessories** (like Adhesives and Grouts).
* **Product Details:** Every product features a high-quality image, a detailed description, and a clear price in South African Rands (ZAR).

### 2. Creating an Account
* **Registration:** Before making a purchase, a customer creates an account by providing their Name, Email, and a Password. This ensures that their order history is safely linked to them and not lost.
* **Forgot Password:** If a customer ever forgets their password months down the line, they simply click "Forgot Password". The system immediately emails them a secure, temporary reset link so they can get back into their account without needing any help from your staff.

### 3. The Shopping Cart
* Much like a physical store, customers can add different tiles to their virtual cart. They can open the cart at any time to increase the number of square meters (or boxes) they need, and the system instantly updates their subtotal.

### 4. The Checkout Process (Step-by-Step)
Once a customer is ready to buy, they enter the Checkout area. This was designed to be completely frictionless.

**Step A: Delivery vs Pickup**
The system will ask how the customer wants to receive their goods:
* **🚚 Deliver to Me:** If they choose delivery, they must type in their street address and select their City/Town from a dropdown menu of 100 Gauteng locations. The system is smart enough to know how far away they are—for example, if they select Midrand, it charges R250. If they select Soweto, it charges R950. This delivery fee is automatically added to their total.
* **🏬 In-Store Pickup (Free):** If they choose to pick it up themselves, the address fields politely disappear, and the delivery fee instantly drops to R0.00. The system notes that they will arrive at your Clayville headquarters to collect the goods.

**Step B: Payment Method**
* **Direct Bank Transfer (EFT):** The system displays strict instructions to the customer, providing the exact Naeve Construction Company banking details (Account Number: **`1769335518`**). It informs them that their order will **not** be processed or shipped until funds have cleared in your account.

**Step C: The Receipt**
* Once they click "Place Order", they immediately get a success screen with an Order Reference Number (e.g. `NCC-12345678`).
* They can click a button to download a professional **PDF Invoice** for their records. 
* At this exact moment, the system also immediately pings the Administrator's email to notify you that a new order has just arrived!

## Part 2: The Administrator Experience (Product Owner Guide)

As the business owner, you have special privileges. When you log into the website using your Admin account (e.g., `admin1@ncctiles.co.za`), the website unlocks a secret **"Dashboard"** button in the navigation bar. This is your command center.

### 1. Fulfilling Customer Orders
When you click on your Dashboard, you will see a list of every order ever placed on the website.

* **Reviewing an Order:** You can open an order to see exactly who bought what. You will see the customer's name, their phone number, whether they wanted Delivery or In-Store Pickup, and exactly how many tiles they requested.
* **Updating Order Statuses:** Next to every order is a dropdown menu representing the state of the order. New orders always start as **"Pending Payment"**.
* **Automated Customer Emails:** This is the most powerful feature. When the customer's EFT payment clears in your bank, you can change their order status in your dashboard from "Pending Payment" to **"Paid"** or **"Processing"**. The moment you click save, the system **automatically engineers a beautiful branded email** and sends it to the customer. When the delivery truck leaves, you update the status to **"Out for Delivery"**, and the customer gets another instant email. You never have to manually type emails to customers again!
* **Deleting Dead Orders:** If a customer simply never pays, or if you were just placing test orders, you can hit the "Delete" button to permanently scrub the order from the record book and keep your dashboard clean.

### 2. Managing the Product Catalog
Your product catalog is a living entity. If prices increase due to inflation, or if you bring in a new line of Italian porcelain, you can manage it all directly from the Dashboard.

* **Add a New Product:** Click the "Add Product" button. You simply type in the name of the new tile, the description, choose a category, enter the price (e.g. 299.99), and provide an image. The second you click save, it appears live on the storefront for customers across the country to buy.
* **Editing Prices:** If a supplier raises their costs, you can click on any existing product in your dashboard, edit the price box, and hit save. The price update is instantaneous. 
* **Deleting Products:** If an old tile line is completely discontinued and you will never stock it again, you can simply delete it from the website entirely.

### Summary
The NCC Tiles platform has been engineered to run your business for you. Customers can securely browse real products, generate their own calculated delivery fees or choose free pickup, and capture their own PDF invoices. Meanwhile, you simply sit at the Admin Dashboard, watch the orders roll in, verify the EFT payments in your bank, and update status codes to automatically email the customers. 

It is a complete, end-to-end digital business.
